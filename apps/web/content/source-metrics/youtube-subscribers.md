  Subscribers is the channel total, and YouTube deliberately rounds it above a certain size.

## Why the figure does not move for a while

YouTube abbreviates public subscriber counts, and the Analytics total for
larger channels is rounded too. Above a hundred thousand the number steps in
increments rather than counting by one, so a channel gaining a few hundred
subscribers a day can show the same figure for several days and then jump.

That rounding is why the widget shows the total without a sparkline. A
sparkline built from a rounded series draws steps and reads as though growth
stopped and restarted.

## The total, not the change

This is the current count, not subscribers gained. For the change over a
window, use the new subscribers widget, which reports gross gains. Neither
one is net: YouTube reports gains and losses separately, and a channel can
add two thousand subscribers and lose five hundred in the same week.
