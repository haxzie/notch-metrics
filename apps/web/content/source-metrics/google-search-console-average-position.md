## Lower is better, which makes the sparkline read backwards

A move from 12.4 to 8.1 is an improvement, and the line goes down. That is
the opposite of every other widget in the notch, so the metric is formatted
with one decimal place and no compacting to make clear it is a rank rather
than a count.

## Averaged over impressions, across every query

The figure averages the position of the topmost result for your site on every
impression it received. A single page ranking first for a term nobody
searches and fortieth for a term everybody does will report somewhere in the
middle, describing neither.

It also moves when nothing about your site changed. Ranking fifth for ten new
long-tail queries pulls the average down even though every existing position
held. Treat a shift as a prompt to look at the query breakdown rather than as
a result in itself.

Search Console data settles over two to three days.
