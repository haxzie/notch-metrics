## A ratio over the window

The figure is computed across the whole window rather than by averaging each
day's own ratio. A day with a thousand impressions and a day with ten carry
their real weight, which is the correct behaviour and the reason the headline
sits where the sparkline does not.

## Why a falling CTR can be good news

CTR drops when a page starts ranking for broader terms. More impressions at
worse positions means a smaller share of them convert, and the page is
earning more clicks in total while the percentage falls. Always read CTR with
impressions and average position beside it; alone it is the most
misinterpreted number Search Console publishes.

CTR also falls when Google answers the query on the results page. That is
outside your control and it is not a page quality problem.

The data settles over two to three days, like every Search Console figure.
