## What is counted

Orders created in the window, bucketed by creation time. Cancelled orders are
included, because an order that was placed and then cancelled is a thing that
happened, and excluding it would make a bad day look like an ordinary one.
Draft orders are not counted until they are completed.

Test orders made through Shopify's bogus gateway are counted too. If your
staging work runs against the live store, that is where an unexplained bump
comes from.

## Orders against sales

Sales divided by orders is average order value, which has its own widget.
Place all three in the notch and they explain each other: sales climbing on
flat orders is a basket getting bigger, and sales climbing on flat average
order value is simply more customers.
