## Ads only

YouTube's estimated revenue includes advertising, channel memberships, Super
Chat, Super Thanks and YouTube Premium watch-time revenue. This figure is the
advertising part alone, from the Analytics API's `estimatedAdRevenue`.

Keeping the two apart is worth a widget. A channel whose total revenue is
flat while ad revenue falls is being carried by memberships, and that is a
different business from the one the total implies.

## Estimated, and it settles

Every YouTube revenue figure is marked estimated for a reason. It is adjusted
for invalid traffic and for advertiser disputes after the fact, and the last
few days of any window move. The month closes properly only once YouTube
finalises it, which is usually well into the following month.

Amounts are reported in your AdSense account's currency, as integer minor
units. Buckets are Pacific Time.
