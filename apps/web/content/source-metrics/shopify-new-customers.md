## First order, not first account

Shopify records a customer when an order is placed, whether or not anybody
created an account. The widget counts customers whose first order falls in
the window, so somebody who browsed for a month and bought today is new today.

A customer who buys again later is not counted again. That is what separates
this from the orders widget and what makes the pair readable: orders rising
faster than new customers means your existing buyers are coming back.

## The limit worth knowing

Shopify identifies a returning customer by email and by account. A shopper
who checks out as a guest with a different email every time is a new customer
every time, which quietly inflates this number on stores with no account
requirement. Read the direction rather than the absolute figure.
