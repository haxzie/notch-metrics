## A ratio, not a sum

YouTube divides total watch time by views over the window. Because it is a
ratio over the whole window rather than an average of the daily ratios, the
headline figure is not the average of the points in the sparkline. A single
day with many short views pulls the headline down more than it pulls any
individual point.

## Reading it against video length

The raw number means nothing without the length of what you published. Four
minutes is excellent on a six-minute video and poor on a forty-minute one.
Watch the direction rather than the value, and watch it in the weeks after a
format change.

A sharp drop usually means the opening is wrong rather than the subject.
Viewers leave in the first thirty seconds more than anywhere else.

Buckets are Pacific Time.
