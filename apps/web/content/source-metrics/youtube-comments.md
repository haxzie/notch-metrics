## What is counted

Comments added in the window, bucketed by Pacific day. Replies are included.
Comments held for review or removed by your own moderation settings are not,
so a channel with strict filters reports lower than its comment section
suggests.

## Reading it

Comments are slow and small compared with likes, and that is what makes them
useful. A viewer who types a sentence has spent real effort. A video with few
views and many comments has found the people who care about the subject,
which is usually worth more than reach.

A sudden climb with no matching rise in views is almost always an argument
rather than an audience. Look before you optimise for it.
