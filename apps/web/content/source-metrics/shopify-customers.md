## A lifetime total, not a window

Every other Shopify widget reports activity inside a window. This one reports
the size of the customer list as it stands, so the window selector does not
change it and the widget carries no sparkline.

It counts every customer record the store holds, including people who ordered
once in 2021 and never returned. It is a measure of how many people have ever
bought, not of how many still would.

## What to place beside it

New customers, for the flow into this total. The ratio between the two over a
month is roughly how fast the list is growing, and a large total with a thin
flow is a store living on its past.

Deleted customer records are gone from this figure. Shopify removes them
rather than marking them, so a GDPR erasure request reduces a number that
otherwise never falls.
