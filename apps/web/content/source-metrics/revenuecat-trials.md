  Active trials are people inside a free trial right now, which makes this the earliest number in a mobile subscription funnel.

## A snapshot, not a count of starts

The figure is how many trials are open at this moment, not how many began in
the window. It rises when more people start trials and falls when they
convert or lapse, so a flat number can hide a healthy flow in both
directions.

Read it as a queue depth. A trial figure that doubles is a cohort arriving
that will decide, one way or the other, within the trial length Apple or
Google is enforcing.

## Why it does not have a sparkline

RevenueCat reports the current state of the project rather than a daily
series of it, so the widget shows the figure as it stands. For movement, put
the active subscriptions widget beside it: trials falling while active
subscriptions rise is conversion, and trials falling while subscriptions hold
flat is not.

Store trial lengths differ, so a trial figure on a project shipping to both
App Store and Play Store mixes two clocks.
